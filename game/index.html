✈ Meridian Courier

Island-hopping cargo flights in a little prop plane

You are the courier pilot of the Meridian Isles. Take off from the runway, thread the glowing rings, deliver the cargo and put her down gently on the next airstrip — before the fuel runs out. Bank too hard and you bleed speed; fly too slow and the wing stalls.

How to fly

Take off: hold Shift to spool the throttle to full, then pull back (↓ or S) once you pass 60 kt. The wheels leave the runway around 30 m/s.

Turns: bank with ← → — the plane turns toward the low wing. Level the wings to fly straight. A steep bank at low speed is a stall waiting to happen.

Rings: fly through the glowing hoop. The gold beacon pillar marks your next objective from anywhere in the sky.

Landing: slow below 100 kt, line up with the runway, descend gently (under 800 ft/min) and touch down wings-level. A hard landing damages the airframe; a very hard one ends the sortie.

Fuel & damage: full throttle burns fuel fast. Empty tanks mean a glide — pick a runway. Deliveries refuel you by 25%.
